# Texturas planas para los modelos 3D

Se usó la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Los dos atlas se copiaron al proyecto como materiales opacos; sus cuatro regiones se aplican a las caras y tapas de los modelos con coordenadas UV.

## Heno — assets/arena/meadow_hay_materials_v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: FLAT MATERIAL TEXTURE ATLAS for UV mapping low polygon 3D hay bale models in a retro PS1 kart game. Produce one square image divided into EXACTLY four equally sized square tiles in a regular 2x2 grid. The texture must fill every pixel of the square. Top left tile: close-up golden straw fibers densely packed, mostly horizontal, with TWO dark brown parallel twine straps running VERTICALLY at one-third and two-thirds of this tile's width, straps straight across the full tile height. Top right tile: a FLAT orthographic circular end pattern of a round hay bale, one broad spiral of tightly wound golden wheat straw perfectly centered and filling the square, matching straw in the four corners too; no silhouette or transparent exterior. Bottom left tile: dense golden straw fibers with TWO brown parallel twine straps running HORIZONTALLY at one-third and two-thirds of this tile's height, straps perfectly straight edge to edge. Bottom right tile: coarse golden yellow straw fiber material without any straps. ALL FOUR are flat surface textures photographed or painted straight on, no perspective, no object depictions, no side faces, no objects casting shadows, no background, no transparency, no gutters, no borders, no grid lines, no text, no numbers. Coarse pixelated low-resolution PS1 hand-painted game texture, restrained ochre and wheat gold palette, evenly lit without directional baked lighting, readable straw fibers, correct continuous bands. This is NOT a picture of hay bales: it is only their FLAT surface materials for wrapping actual cube and cylinder meshes.

## Madera — assets/arena/meadow_logs_materials_v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: FLAT MATERIAL TEXTURE ATLAS for UV mapping low polygon 3D tree log cylinder meshes in a retro PS1 kart game. Produce one square image divided into exactly FOUR equally sized square tiles in a regular 2x2 grid, every pixel fully filled. Top left tile: dark warm brown rough tree bark, long vertical grooves, coarse rectangular bark chips, perfectly FLAT straight-on material texture, seamless-looking edge-to-edge. Top right tile: golden tan cut wood end grain, concentric growth rings perfectly centered in the square, a few short radial cracks, rings fill entire tile including the corners, no visible cylindrical sides. Bottom left tile: a second coarse bark texture, slightly lighter earthy brown with long vertical grain grooves and dark fissures. Bottom right tile: a second flat circular cut end grain pattern, warm pale tan rings, small natural cracks, perfectly centered and filling every pixel of the square. Flat orthographic material swatches only, no perspective, no depiction of whole logs, no silhouettes, no scenic background, no cast shadows, no transparent pixels, no gutters, no borders or grid lines, no labels or numbers. Even neutral illumination without directional baked lighting. Chunky hand-painted coarse pixelated texture matching low-poly PlayStation 1 environment art, natural earthy browns and warm muted tan wood. The bark tiles will wrap the curved sides of cylinders; the circular end grain tiles will be used only on their flat circular caps.
